# PRODUCT.md — lowyap

- **Product:** `lowyap` (`com.lowyap.desktop` · `https://lowyap.com` · `github.com/lowyap/lowyap`)
- **Tagline:** `"lowkey yap. it types."`
- **Platform:** Native macOS Studio & Floating Pill (`Tauri v2` + `Rust` + `Metal4` / `Accelerate` + `React 19`), with cross-platform Windows/Linux support.
- **Core Job:** Hold a shortcut (`⌥ Space`), speak naturally in 100+ languages (including bilingual Hinglish/Urdu), watch live chunks appear in a tactile floating pill while speaking, and paste clean, structured prose into any app with `0 B` cloud audio egress by default — plus bot-free dual-channel (`Mic + System Audio`) meeting notes stored in a local SQLite vault.
- **Audience:** Builders, engineers, founders, researchers, and vocal thinkers across Gen-Z and modern professionals who think out loud faster than they type, hate meeting bots joining their calls, and refuse to upload every keystroke and conversation to a cloud SaaS server.
- **Brand Archetype:** **"Vocal Hardware"** (`Teenage Engineering OP-1 Field × Linear × modern vocal culture`) — tactile, machined, calm, unapologetically lowercase, zero corporate bloat, zero AI-sparkle purple.

## Two-Axis Quality & Brand Rubric (`80` Points Total)

Every surface (macOS Studio, Floating Pill, Onboarding, and `lowyap.com`) is evaluated on **two independent 40-point axes** so functional cleanliness never masks generic brand execution:

1. **Axis 1 — Functional UX & Accessibility (`40 pts`):**
   - Information Architecture & progressive disclosure (`10`)
   - WCAG AA contrast (`>= 4.5:1` text, `>= 3:1` UI) in both Light & Dark (`10`)
   - Real-time responsiveness, keyboard/RTL parity & honest state feedback (`10`)
   - Copy clarity & zero deceptive claims (`10`)
2. **Axis 2 — Brand & Tactile Soul (`40 pts`):**
   - **Logo & Symbol Craft (`10`):** Custom Vocal Keycap mark (3 stepped acoustic bars + Signal Vermilion `_` cursor) & optically balanced `lowyap_` wordmark; zero stock icons or cartoon mascots.
   - **Typography Voice (`10`):** Bundled `SIL OFL-1.1` triad (`Bricolage Grotesque Variable` display + `Hanken Grotesk Variable` UI + `Azeret Mono Variable` hardware telemetry); zero generic `SF Pro Rounded` / `Roboto` leaks.
   - **Material & Color Cohesion (`10`):** Anodized Warm Stone (`#F4F2EC`) + Machined Carbon (`#111113`) + Signal Vermilion (`#C0320C` / `#FF6B3D`) synchronized 1:1 across app, pill, tray, and web.
   - **Signature Tactile Delight (`10`):** Physical keycap geometry, live waveform-to-text sculpting, millisecond stage telemetry, and honest `0 B sent` hardware indicators.
